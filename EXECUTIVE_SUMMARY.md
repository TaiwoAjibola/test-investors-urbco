# Urbco Investors — Executive Summary

> Confidential overview of the Urbco Investors platform and its two investment products
> ("projects"): **Urbco Harbour** (fractional) and **Urbco Foundry** (institutional / high-value).
> Source: the live Urbco Investors App (Next.js, React, TypeScript).

---

## Platform Overview

**Urbco Investors** is a digital real-estate investment platform that lets people and institutions
co-own income-generating property in Nigeria. Instead of buying a whole building, investors buy a
fraction of a vetted asset and earn proportional rental income (dividends) plus potential capital
appreciation. The platform handles discovery, KYC, payments, custody/title, and dividend distribution
end-to-end, with a dedicated admin settlement pipeline behind every asset.

Two distinct products sit on the platform, separated by ticket size and investor type:

| | **Urbco Harbour** | **Urbco Foundry** |
|---|---|---|
| Model | Fractional ownership | High-value / co-investment |
| Minimum commitment | ₦100,000 | ₦200,000,000 |
| Who can join | Individuals only | HNW individuals, family offices, institutions |
| Best for | First-time & retail investors | Wealthy individuals & institutions |
| Typical assets | Residential, student housing, hospitality | Commercial, mixed-use, industrial, infrastructure |

## Project 1 — Urbco Harbour (Fractional)

### Details of the app / what it does
Urbco Harbour opens premium real estate to everyday investors. A user signs up as an **Individual**,
completes individual KYC (Government Photo ID, biometric selfie, proof of address, accreditation
questionnaire + source of funds), funds a wallet, and browses a marketplace of fractional assets.
They can invest from as little as **₦100,000**, track performance in a portfolio view, and receive
rental dividends paid into their wallet. A referral programme rewards users for bringing new investors.

### How it can be applied
- **Retail wealth building:** give salary earners exposure to real estate without ₦50M+ capital.
- **Diaspora investment:** Nigerians abroad can co-own Nigerian property remotely, fully KYC'd.
- **Savings alternative:** a higher-yield, asset-backed alternative to fixed deposits.
- **Financial inclusion:** lowers the barrier to a historically illiquid asset class.

### Sectors it can be applied to
Residential · Student Housing · Hospitality (serviced apartments / short-let) · Mixed-Use ·
select Commercial. These are chosen for steady rental demand and lower ticket sizes.

## Project 2 — Urbco Foundry (High-Value / Institutional)

### Details of the app / what it does
Urbco Foundry is the institutional and high-net-worth track. Investors choose **HNW Individual**,
**Family Office**, or **Institution** at sign-up and complete a heavier compliance flow
(institutional KYB: CAC registration, beneficial-ownership/UBO, board resolution, AML certificate,
officer IDs, AUM proof). Minimum commitment is **₦200,000,000**. Foundry supports bespoke
co-investment structures (Real Estate Fund, Private Equity / co-investment, Direct Asset Holdco,
SPV / Consortium) with configurable ticket sizes, investment horizons, and preferred sectors.

### How it can be applied
- **Institutional allocation:** pension funds, banks/DFIs, insurers, and investment managers deploy
  real-estate sleeves without building in-house property teams.
- **Family-office diversification:** allocate private capital to vetted, income-producing assets.
- **Co-investment & SPVs:** syndicate large deals across multiple institutions via consortium SPVs.
- **Treasury management:** corporates park surplus cash in yield-bearing real estate.

### Sectors it can be applied to
Commercial · Mixed-Use · Industrial & Logistics · Hospitality · Student Housing · Healthcare ·
Infrastructure · Residential. Selected for scale, yield, and institutional suitability.

## Cross-Cutting Capabilities (both projects)

- **Marketplace** of vetted assets with stage, ROI, track and buying-path badges.
- **KYC / KYB** per user type (individual, family office, institution) with submit → review →
  verified / remediation workflow.
- **Wallet** for deposits, withdrawals, and dividend receipt.
- **Portfolio** analytics (performance charts, invested vs returns).
- **Dividends** tracking (paid / upcoming / projected).
- **Referrals** with rewards.
- **Admin settlement pipeline** behind each asset: Terms & Allocation → Payment & Settlement →
  Custody & Title → Reconciliation → Release (with deeper 9-step admin lifecycle).

## Strategic Value

Urbco turns a ₦100K saver and a ₦200M institution into co-owners of the same asset class, on one
compliant platform — expanding the investor base, deepening liquidity, and professionalising
real-estate access in Nigeria and for the diaspora.
